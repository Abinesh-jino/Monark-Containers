What services does Monark offer?

Monark offers container trading, sales and supply, rental and leasing, global sourcing, and logistics coordination. Share your requirements and our team can help identify the right service.

Which container types and sizes do you offer?

We offer 20 ft and 40 ft Standard Dry, High Cube, Reefer, Open Top and Flat Rack containers. Our team can confirm the suitable type, condition and availability for your requirements.

Can I rent a container for the short or long term?

Yes. Short-term and long-term rental options are available for storage, commercial and project requirements. Share your container type, location and rental period so we can discuss availability and terms.

Do you arrange container delivery?

We coordinate container delivery and transportation across the UAE through logistics partners. Share your collection point, destination and site details so we can discuss suitable arrangements.

How do I request a quote?

Use our Contact form, email Contactus@Monark.ae or call +971 55 205 2260. Include the service, container type and size, quantity, location and preferred timeframe.

Do you support international sourcing and trade requirements?

Yes. MONARK helps businesses source products, suppliers and trading opportunities across international markets, with support based on your specific requirement and destination.

Can MONARK handle custom or bulk container requirements?

Yes. We can support both single-container and bulk requirements, including specific container types, sizes, quantities and project-based needs depending on availability.
